### About

Computer Science (AI & ML) undergrad at Vishwakarma Institute of Information Technology, Pune — building full-stack products and AI-powered backend systems, not just tutorials. Smart India Hackathon National Nominee, finalist at Project Morpheus, and lead of a 75+ member campus team. Currently shipping **FrontWing**, a multi-agent F1 analytics platform, fresh off an internship building real-time computer-vision proctoring systems at My WebRide Technologies.

I like building things where the backend does something genuinely hard — orchestrating agents, scoring risk in real time, moving money or trust between systems that don't inherently agree with each other.
